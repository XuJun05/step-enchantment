**Step Enchantment** is a lightweight and vanilla-friendly enchantment mod designed for Fabric. It introduces the **Step** enchantment for boots, allowing players to smoothly step up blocks and steep terrain without jumping.

> **Notice**: This mod is currently in active development. Please report any issues or suggestions on our GitHub repository.

## Features

* **Custom Step Enchantment**: Adds the "Step" enchantment exclusively for boots (feet armor).
* **Progressive Step Heights**:
  * **Level I**: Step up **1 block** (walk up full blocks smoothly without jumping).
  * **Level II**: Step up **2 blocks** (easily climb up 2-block obstacles and ledges).
  * **Level III**: Step up **3 blocks** (effortlessly scale steep hills, mountains, and walls).
* **Survival Friendly Acquisition**:
  * **Enchanting Table**: Available directly from enchanting boots at an enchanting table.
  * **Villager Trading**: Trade with Librarian villagers for enchanted books.
  * **Fishing**: Catch enchanted books while fishing.
  * **Loot Chests**: Generated in dungeon and structure chests as regular enchanted loot.
* **Multi-Language Support**: Fully localized in English, Japanese, and Chinese.

## Installation & Requirements

Step Enchantment **can be installed on both Server and Client**:

1. Requires **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**.
2. Place Step Enchantment into the `mods` folder of your Minecraft directory.
3. Launch Minecraft and enjoy smooth climbing!
